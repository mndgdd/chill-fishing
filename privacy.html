🎣 Chill Fishing — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Thank you for playing Chill Fishing ("the Game"). This policy explains what data the Game collects, why, and how it is handled.

✅ Short version: No login required. We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, or GPS location. The only data we store are your chosen nickname, a random anonymous ID, and your fishing scores — used only for the in-game leaderboard and cloud save.

1. Data We Collect (Our Servers)

When you use the leaderboard or cloud save feature, the following data is sent to our servers (hosted on Cloudflare):

  • Player nickname — the name you choose in the game (up to 16 characters). This is displayed on the leaderboard. You can use any nickname; it does not need to be your real name.
  • Anonymous game ID — a random string generated on first launch, stored on your device. Used to identify your cloud save and leaderboard entry. It is not tied to your real identity.
  • Game progress — gold earned, level, fish caught, and upgrades. Used to display your rank on the leaderboard and to sync your save across devices.
  • IP address — automatically logged by Cloudflare for security and rate-limiting purposes. Not linked to your nickname or game data.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

The Game does not collect or transmit:

  • Your real name, email address, phone number, or any account login
  • Your GPS location or precise location
  • Your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera
  • Your browsing history or any data outside the Game

3. Data Collected by Google AdMob (Third-Party SDK)

The Game shows advertisements (rewarded videos for extra gold, and occasional banner ads) via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Device advertising ID (for ad personalization and frequency capping)
  • Approximate IP address (rough country/region for ad serving)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Display your nickname and score on the leaderboard (Top 100 by gold)
  • Sync your game progress across devices (cloud save)
  • Improve game stability and prevent leaderboard abuse

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties except Google AdMob (for ads) and Cloudflare (for hosting).

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your game data is stored on our servers until you request deletion. To delete your data, email us at wcbflsg@gmail.com with your game ID or nickname, and we will remove it within 7 days. Uninstalling the Game from your device does not automatically delete server-side leaderboard data.

6. Children's Privacy

Chill Fishing is designed for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. AdMob is configured to serve non-personalized ads in compliance with COPPA and Google Play Families policy.

7. Offline Play

You can play the entire game offline. The Game only contacts the network when you:

  • Watch a rewarded ad (AdMob)
  • View or submit to the leaderboard (our servers)
  • Sync your cloud save (our servers)

8. Data Security

All network requests use HTTPS encryption. Our database (Cloudflare D1) stores only the anonymous game ID, nickname, and game scores. There is no real-name data, no financial information, and no passwords.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above.
