🎣 Chill Fishing — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Thank you for playing Chill Fishing ("the Game"). We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data the Game does and does not collect when you use it.

✅ Short version: Chill Fishing does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or any personal account. You can play the entire game offline without logging in.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

The Game does not collect or transmit:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or any account information
  • Your GPS location or precise location
  • Your contacts, photos, or media files
  • Your device ID, advertising ID, or any persistent identifier
  • Your browsing history or any data outside the Game

All game progress (gold, fish caught, level, upgrades) is stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the Game erases this data.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party SDKs

The Game uses Google AdMob to show advertisements (rewarded ads for extra gold, and occasional banner ads). AdMob may collect certain data as described in Google's privacy policy, including:

  • Device advertising ID (for ad personalization and frequency capping)
  • Approximate IP address (used to determine rough country/region for ad serving)
  • App interaction data (ad impressions, clicks)

You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

For more details, see Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Children's Privacy

Chill Fishing is designed for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. AdMob is configured to serve non-personalized ads in compliance with COPPA and Google Play Families policy.

4. Offline Play

You can play the entire game offline. No data is sent to our servers when you play without an internet connection. The Game only contacts the network to load ads.

5. Data Security

Because all game data stays on your device, there is no server database to breach. The only network requests are to Google AdMob's ad servers over HTTPS.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above.
